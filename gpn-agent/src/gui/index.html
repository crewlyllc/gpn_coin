Your referral code

derived from your wallet

Every node has one, and it is not chosen — it is computed from this node's payout wallet, so the same wallet always gives the same code after a reinstall. Share the link when you invite someone; it tells the network who they came from, nothing more.

Second coin — your own, beside GPN

off

Mine a second coin on the same card while GPN works. It runs at reduced intensity and stops for every AI job. The payout address is yours: these rewards never count as GPN earnings and never enter the airdrop record. Only coins whose DAG fits this card are listed.

 

Solana staking — your SOL, your keys

off

Delegate your own SOL from a keypair on this machine. Rewards go to your own stake account and are never counted as GPN earnings or in the airdrop. Deactivating takes 1–3 days at an epoch boundary, so the panel shows the date instead of a spinner.

 

Other earnings

Rewards from the second coin and from staking, kept separate from GPN's own earnings. This panel appears only while one of them is active.
