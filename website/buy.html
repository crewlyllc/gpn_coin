Where GPN is today — and where it's going

Straight answer: GPN is on a public testnet. There is no live token market yet, and testnet GPN has no monetary value. Here's how to get it now, and what the mainnet plan looks like.

Testnet today · not listed · not investment advice

GPN is running on a public testnet. Testnet tokens are for testing and carry no monetary value, and nothing on this page is an offer, solicitation, or investment advice. Do not expect to buy or sell GPN at a market price today.

🚰 Faucet

Drip testnet GPN to a fresh account to pay registration fees and test the network. On the testnet, the public access point can also pay escrow for you in free-AI mode, so a client spends nothing.

⛏ Mining

Run a node and mine. Because rewards are Bitcoin-style, the node that produces a block keeps that block's reward — paid to your own account. This is the fair-launch distribution path.

✦ Job earnings

Serve AI jobs as a provider and earn GPN from job escrow. Buyers pay per token; providers are paid on settlement (minus the 5% marketplace fee).

Roadmap

The mainnet listing plan

Listing is deliberately deferred until after mainnet launch and initial stability. The current plan is a CEX/DEX liquidity and market-maker effort — not an on-chain feature. No listing date is promised.

Mainnet launch

Genesis with the fair-launch emission path and an inert sudo; the token goes live on-chain (not yet on an exchange).

Stability first

Confirm the chain and marketplace are stable before any market-making or listing work begins — deliberately sequenced, not rushed.

Liquidity & listings

A market-maker and CEX/DEX listing effort so GPN becomes tradable. This is operational and out of the repo's scope today; it will be announced when real.

Until then, the only way to obtain GPN is to earn it on testnet. Anyone offering to sell you GPN before an official announcement is not us.

Tokenomics

Fair launch, disclosed treasury, halving

🌱 Fair launch

No ICO and no liquid premine. All GPN is minted per block, and halving is the sole emission mechanism. Distribution happens by mining, not by a sale.

📉 Halving

The block reward halves every 4,204,800 blocks — roughly every two years at a 15-second block time. Current reward: 50 GPN/block (≈ 3.33 GPN/s).

🏛 Treasury & vesting

A disclosed 100 M GPN treasury — 70 M genesis-unlocked + 30 M vested over 10 years (20 × 1.5 M every 6 months). It is held by a keyless pallet account and spendable only via a 13-of-25 governance roster. Its mandate is demand-generation (listings, liquidity, developer relations, onboarding) — not a founder payout.

🧮 Supply

Nominal cap 1 B GPN, but the implemented emission path yields roughly 420 M mineable, giving an effective max supply of ≈ 520 M including the treasury. Fees: a 5% marketplace fee splits 40% staking / 30% treasury / 20% burn / 10% oracle operators.

Why the token should be worth something

The oracle seeds a GPN/USD price for the testnet, but the token's long-run value is intended to derive from real AI-marketplace demand — not just mining inflation. That's the thesis, not a guarantee.
