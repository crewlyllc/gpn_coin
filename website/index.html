The problem

Two ways to earn, and you have to pick one

Owning a GPU has always presented two, siloed markets for the same silicon. They were never connected — so the hardware can't move to where the money is.

① Mine a proof-of-work coin

You're paid whatever the coin is worth today. Coin dips and you mine at a loss — the card keeps hashing because it can't see anything else.

×

② Rent the GPU for AI work

You're paid whatever compute is worth today. List it and nobody books you, and it earns exactly nothing while it idles.

Meanwhile

AI compute is expensive and concentrated: buyers pay hyperscaler prices, while consumer GPUs — the cheapest compute on earth — sit idle.

GPN's answer

Don't make the owner pick. Let the GPU work both markets.

GPN is a network where each GPU mines or serves AI, auto-switching to whichever earns more — and where AI buyers get a single interface that routes their work to that same pool of GPUs. A thin chain handles coordination, escrow, and settlement; the heavy compute runs off-chain.

How it works

Mine, serve, route, settle

One install gives you a node that mines on its GPU and can serve AI jobs. A buyer hits one endpoint; the network does the rest.

⛏ Mining

GPU proof-of-work (Ethash-derived, memory-hard). The node that produces a block keeps that block's reward — Bitcoin-style. Eligibility is GPU-only, verified by work, not by a permissioned validator set.

✦ Serving AI

LLM inference, image/video generation, ML inference, and fine-tuning — priced per token against real cost, with per-job escrow and an automatic surplus refund.

⇄ The switch

Each cycle the node compares mining vs. compute revenue in USD/hr from an on-chain oracle feed and flips when one beats the other by more than ~5% — so it doesn't thrash on tiny price crossings.

🧭 Routing

Buyers hit a single access point. The scheduler filters by capability, requires an idle GPU, and picks the lowest-latency, least-loaded verified provider — discovered on-chain, not from a hand-maintained list.

🔒 Escrow & settlement

Funds are escrowed on-chain and released on delivery. Optional payment channels lock funds once and settle each job off-chain, so the AI path has no per-job block delay.

🕶 Privacy

A shielded pool (Sapling-style commitments + nullifiers) supports private transfers and withdrawals. The chain sees job specs and result hashes — not prompts, models, or outputs.

What you get

Built to be earned, not trusted

₿ Bitcoin-style rewards

  • Producer keeps the block reward — a coinbase model, no shared beneficiary.
  • Per-node wallets — each miner's own account is its payout address.
  • Self-tuning difficulty; 15-second blocks; a ~2-year halving.

🎛 GPU-only, verifiably

  • Memory-hard PoW makes CPUs non-competitive by construction.
  • Attestation tiers (Bronze/Silver/Gold) raise your reward multiplier.
  • Stake-free path: producing a valid block is proof of real GPU work.

🤝 Trust-minimal

  • On-chain escrow; the chain is the root of trust.
  • Optional payment channels — no per-job block delay.
  • Committee governance (13-of-25), timelocked; an immutable genesis.

🕶 Privacy

  • Shielded pool: hidden notes, zero-knowledge spends, unlinkable nullifiers.
  • Prompts, models, and outputs stay off-chain.
  • On-chain values are salted/opaque.
