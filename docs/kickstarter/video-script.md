# GPN — Campaign Video Script (60–90s)

**Target length:** 75s (cut-downs: 30s social, 15s bumper).
**Tone:** plain-spoken, honest, concrete. No hype, no price talk, no "to the moon."
**Tagline in-video and on the end card:** *A GPU should not have to choose.*
**Legal/honesty guardrails:** say **"could earn,"** never "will earn"; never mention a price,
listing, or return; no tokens are sold.

## B-roll and pickup ideas

- **Idle hardware:** a workstation at night, monitor asleep, GPU LED breathing. Timelapse of a day
  passing while the card sits still.
- **The hive:** a shelf/closet of GPUs (even a modest 2–3 card rig) — "the cheapest compute on
  earth, already paid for."
- **Hands:** someone plugging a card in, clicking "Start," a fan spinning up — tactile, human.
- **Real math on a real screen:** the earnings calculator, the USD/hr comparison bar in the
  dashboard (blur anything that looks like a price promise).
- **Textural inserts:** thermal paste, a screwdriver, RGB off/on — keep it grounded, not glossy.
- **Testnet proof:** a short screen-capture montage of the explorer, a completed AI job, and a
  shielded spend — with a caption *"live on testnet."*
- **Honesty insert (optional):** a simple on-screen card reading *"Phase 1: the switch oracle is
  centralized. We're engineering the path off it."* — ready to paste over any beat.

## Music & pacing notes

- Instrumental only; a low, steady pulse that "switches" tempo with the mode change (mining A →
  compute B). No drop-heavy EDM, no hype piano.
- Sound design: fan spin-down/up, a soft "click" on each mode switch, keyboard/token-stream ticks.
- Captions: burned-in; assume the video plays muted in social feeds.

## Platform cut-downs

- **30s (social):** Hook (0:00–0:08) → Problem in one line → the dashboard switch (shot 6) →
  honesty beat → ask.
- **15s (bumper):** GPU idle → "Mine or serve AI?" → the switch → tagline + link.
- **6s (loop/CTA):** dashboard flipping MINING ⇄ SERVING AI with the tagline.
