# GPN — Juicebox Project Copy

*Copy for a Juicebox project page. Juicebox is a permissionless on-chain fundraising protocol:
a project sets a funding target and options, contributors pay in (ETH), and the treasury holds the
funds with programmable rules. This draft is a **no-token raise** (see the reward note) to avoid
securities complications.*

## Project name
**GPN — Mine or Serve AI With One GPU**

## Tagline

## Short description (project card)
GPN turns any GPU into income two ways — it can **mine** the network's proof-of-work token, or **serve
AI compute** — and **auto-switches** to whichever pays more. Help us ship mainnet, audits, and the
trusted-setup ceremony. Open-source, trust-minimal, fair-launch.

## Rewards / tokens — **recommended: none**
**Recommendation: do NOT issue a transferable token via Juicebox.** A token-for-money raise is the
exact securities-risk surface that gets projects in trouble. Options:
- **A pure donation raise** — contributors get **recognition**, not a claim (a supporter wall / role in
  the community, an on-chain "thanks" event). Safest.
- **A non-transferable "supporter" badge** (soulbound) — recognition only, clearly stated as
  **non-financial, no return, no claim on the treasury**.
- **If** you ever do issue a project token, do it **with counsel** (jurisdiction, KYC/AML, exemptions)
  — not impulsively.

**Explicit line to include:** *"Contributions are donations. You receive no tokens, equity, or
financial return. GPN is not soliciting an investment."*

## Long description
**The problem.** Every GPU owner faces the same two doors and must pick one: **mine** a coin, or
**rent the card** for AI work. Mine and the coin dips → you lose. Rent and nobody books you → $0.
The card can't see that *the other market is paying more right now* and move.

**The idea.** GPN is a GPU-native network where each node **mines _or_ serves AI — auto-switching** to
whichever earns more. Same GPU, one install, no manual re-listing. The chain does **coordination +
escrow + settlement**; the heavy compute runs **off-chain**. Rewards are **Bitcoin-style** (the node
that produces a block keeps its reward). Eligibility is **GPU-only**, enforced by proof-of-work and
hardware attestation. AI jobs are priced **per token** against real cost, escrowed trust-minimally,
and routed to the **nearest, least-loaded** verified GPU.

**Where we are.** Live on a public **testnet**: the PoW chain, per-node rewards, the full AI
marketplace (LLM, image, video, ML, fine-tuning), on-chain provider discovery, payment channels,
shielded privacy, peer discovery, and committee governance. **The code is open-source.**

**What we're funding.** The last mile: **independent audits**, the **trusted-setup ceremony** that
makes the privacy layer production-grade, mainnet readiness, and the seed nodes/CI that keep the
network decentralized and testable.

**Why it matters.** The GPU is the most abundant and most under-used source of compute on earth.
Mining monopolized it; AI rental markets fight over it; neither lets the hardware go where the money
is. **GPN makes one GPU earn from both.**

**Honest caveats (we tell the whole story):** it's software, and GPN is a young network going
testnet → mainnet; timelines carry risk; the privacy layer needs the ceremony to be trustless; a
governance treasury (10%, 13-of-25 roster) is disclosed; the price oracle is Phase‑1 centralized.
